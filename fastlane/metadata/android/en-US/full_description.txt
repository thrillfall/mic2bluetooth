Mic2Bluetooth turns your Android phone into a wireless microphone for any paired Bluetooth speaker, headset, or pair of earbuds. Speak into the phone — hear yourself live on the connected device.

Two routing modes:

* <b>A2DP (default):</b> sends the mic signal as media audio to any Bluetooth speaker. Full audio bandwidth, around 150–250 ms latency due to the Bluetooth audio path.
* <b>Low latency (SCO):</b> uses the Bluetooth HFP profile for headsets and earbuds. Around 40–80 ms latency, narrowband speech quality. Plain speakers without a microphone profile are not supported in this mode.

Two interaction modes:

* <b>Toggle:</b> tap the big red button to start, tap again to stop.
* <b>Hold to speak:</b> press and hold the button for push-to-talk; release to stop.

The app auto-detects Bluetooth devices that connect or disconnect while it is open.

<b>Privacy:</b> no network access, no analytics, no ads, no third-party services. Audio is captured from the microphone and routed through the Android audio framework to the selected output device. Nothing is recorded or transmitted off the device.

<b>Permissions:</b>

* RECORD_AUDIO — capture from the microphone.
* MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS — switch audio routing.
* BLUETOOTH_CONNECT — required only when low-latency (SCO) mode is enabled, to route to a Bluetooth communication device.

<b>Requirements:</b> Android 12 (API 31) or newer.
